***
> Target Languages to Learn: Spanish, French, Norwegian
# Time Allocation
- **Session length:** 15 or 45 minutes.
- **Weekdays:** Minimum of three 15-minute sessions.
    - Mon/Thu: Writing (Gemini)
    - Tue/Fri: Speaking (Tiny Aya + TTS)
    - Wed: Vocabulary review (LingQ)
- **Weekends:** Three 45-minute sessions.
    - 30 min: Reading/listening (LingQ)
    - 15 min: LingQ spaced-repetition review
    - No supplementary resources.

# Learning Resources
- **LingQ:** Grammar, vocabulary, reading, listening, and spaced repetition.
- **Gemini:** Writing exercises and bidirectional translation.
- **Tiny Aya + TTS:** Conversational speaking and listening practice.

# Learning Techniques
- **Bidirectional Translation:** Translate proficiency-matched passages between the native and target language, followed by AI feedback. For each language, get a list of words that you're working on from LingQ by exporting the vocabulary, then upload it so the AI understands your level.
	- Prompt: "By using the uploaded vocabulary list in [type target language], generate 2 different paragraphs in [insert topic of choice, or let the AI choose] for bi-directional translation practice. Ensure that they are appropriate for the level that I am able to speak at. Do not provide the correct translations, or any vocabulary for assistance. Only provide the 2 paragraphs, noting that the entire exercise should take approximately 15 minutes to complete. After my response, provide me a percentage score as to what my accuracy was, for each passage, and very concisely provide specific information as to what I did wrong."
- **Conversation Practice:** Begin with English setup prompts, then transition entirely to the target language.
- **Comprehensible Input:** Read while listening to synchronized audio; unknown vocabulary is reviewed using LingQ flashcards.

# Evaluation
Assess progress **monthly** (first day of the month) using four quantitative metrics:

|Metric|Assessment|Passing Criterion|
|---|---|---|
|Reading/Writing|Bidirectional translation|≥85% accuracy|
|Speaking|AI conversation assessment|≥85% score|
|Listening|Note-taking from spoken passage|≥85% information recalled|
|Vocabulary|LingQ known-word count|CEFR vocabulary targets|

**CEFR Benchmarks**
- **A1:** 1.5k words; simple sentences.
- **A2:** 4k words; short paragraphs and basic conversations.
- **B1:** 8k words; sustained conversations and multi-paragraph texts.
- **B2:** 15k words; abstract discussion and complex texts.
- **C1/C2:** 20k+ words; near-native comprehension and production.
