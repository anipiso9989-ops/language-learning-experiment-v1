> I noticed something interesting today. Although I have done a considerable amount of vocabulary practice on LingQ, my performance on these writing exercises was less than I expected, for Norwegian and French. I did a little better on Spanish because I'm more experienced with it, but still. I think the english → target language aspect of the exercise is hardest, because that especially forces you to synthesize everything you've learned. 
> 
> Spanish 15 minute bi-directional practice was 90% accuracy on Spanish → English, 60% accuracy on English → Spanish. Mistakes on the latter include:
> - missing vocabulary ("alquilar" = to rent, "lease" = contrato, "quiet" = tranquilo)
> - verb tense/grammar issues ("have been saving" = han *estado* ahorrando, used "era" instead of "estaba" in past tense used for emotional state, "tuvieron" instead of "tengan" for preterite tense of a completed action)
> - and gender agreements ("amb*as* opciones" not ambos, and "una decision" not un)
> 
> Norwegian 15-minute bi-directional practice was 78% accuracy on Norwegian → English, 42% on English → Norwegian. Mistakes for N→E include:
> - "på å" = about
> - "mye annet" = a lot of things
> - "neste året" = next year
> Mistakes for E→N include:
> - wake up = "våkner"
> - always = "alltid", not "alle"
> - 8 O'Clock = "klokken åtte", not "åtten i klokken"
> - asked = "ba / spurte"
> - together = "sammen"
> - tired = "trøtt"
> - was ready = "var klart"
> - For some reason, "orange juice" in Norwegian is "applesinjuice"
> - In a main clause, the verb comes second (V2 Word Rule). "Hun liker aldri", not "Hun aldri liker", to say "She doesn't like"
> - Time conventions: "Om kvelden" or "På kvelden" to say "In the evening"
>   
>  French 15-minute bi-directional practice was 70% accuracy on French → English, and 35% on English → French. 
>  Mistakes for F→E include:
>  - "à la campagne" = in the countryside
>  - "frais au marché" = fresh at the market
>  - "joli" = nice/pretty
>  - "Le matin" = in the morning
>  - "poisson" = fish
>  - "sont allés" = were going
>  - "Hier" = yesterday
>  Mistakes for E→F include:
>  - "Today" = Aujourd'hui
>  - "Dress" = Robe
>  - "This gift" = Ce Cadeau
>  - "City" = Ville
>  - "Computer" = Ordinateur
>  - "Last year" was translated as _Le anee prochaine_ ("next year" with a typo) instead of _L'année dernière_.
>  - "loved to go to the store" was translated as _aime a voyager a store_ (present tense, incorrect verb, untranslated "store") instead of _aimait aller au magasin_
>  - "Thus" was translated as _Puis_ ("Then") instead of _Ainsi_
>  - Verb forms/tenses: _era_ (Spanish) instead of _était_ (imperfect); _veux_ instead of _veut_ (3rd person); _Allait-t-elle_ has an extra _-t-_ (_Allait-elle_)
>  - Missing accents & typos: _anee_ (_année_), _a_ (_à_), _espere_ (_espère_), _mere_ (_mère_), _achete_ (_achète_), _affames_ (_affamés_)
>  - Gender/Agreement: _un nouveaux_ with a feminine noun (_une nouvelle robe_); _sa_ before masculine _ordinateur_ (_son ordinateur_)
